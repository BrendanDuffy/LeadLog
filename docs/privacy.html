Privacy Policy for Lead Log

Last updated: September 2026

Lead Log ("the app") is a personal record-keeping tool for logging range sessions and tracking your own firearm and ammunition inventory. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

No data is collected or transmitted

Lead Log does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data. The app has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising software of any kind. Everything you enter, including firearms, ammunition, range session logs, service records, and any photos you attach, is stored only in the app's local database on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage framework.

Camera access

Lead Log requests camera access solely so you can attach photos of targets or firearms to your own log entries. Photos you take are saved locally on your device alongside your other data and are never uploaded anywhere.

No third parties

Lead Log does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. No information about you or your use of the app is shared with anyone, including the developer.

Your data, your control

Because all data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. You can delete individual entries at any time within the app. Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all of its locally stored data, including any photos.

Children's privacy

Lead Log is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, since it collects no information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, for example if a future version of the app adds a new feature that changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
