Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Everything you enter in Lead Log, including firearms, ammo, session logs, service records, and photos, stays on your device only. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
Do I need an account to use Lead Log?
No. There's no sign-in, and the app doesn't require an internet connection to work.
Will I lose my data if I delete the app?
Yes. Since all data is stored locally, deleting the app removes it permanently. Back up your device via iCloud or iTunes/Finder backup as you normally would to protect against this.
Does Lead Log help me buy, sell, or trade firearms or ammunition?
No. Lead Log is strictly a personal record-keeping tool for logging your own range activity and inventory. It has no marketplace, purchasing, or trading features.
Where do I report a bug or a feature request?
Email duffy.brendan820@gmail.com with details and, if possible, steps to reproduce.
